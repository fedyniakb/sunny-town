Make your own character and move into Sunny Town. Get a job at the café, decorate your house room by room, ride the Ferris wheel, go fishing, read storybooks at the library, learn at the school, and find shiny treasures for the museum.

No ads. No chats with strangers. No tracking. Coins are earned by playing.
