Privacy Policy

Sunny Town for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 10, 2026.
HomeSupport

Short version: we don't collect any information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. Sunny Town does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts to create in the game, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (your character, house, coins and so on) is saved on the device.
  • iCloud: if iCloud is turned on, progress is also kept in your own iCloud account so it carries over to your other Apple devices. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage; we cannot see or access it.
  • In-game photos taken with the game's camera are saved on the device only.
  • Names you type (your character's name, your pets' names) stay in your saved game on your devices.

Playing together (optional, off by default)

A grown-up can turn on playing together in Menu, For grown-ups. There are two ways:

  • In this home: devices on the same Wi-Fi or close by connect directly to each other using Apple's peer-to-peer networking. Nothing goes through the internet or our servers.
  • Online: uses Apple's Game Center. Players who enter the same four-digit world code join the same town. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you need to be signed in to Game Center in the device's Settings. Grown-ups can limit or turn off multiplayer games for a child with Screen Time, under Content & Privacy Restrictions, Game Center.

While playing together, the only things sent to the other players are the character's name, look, where they are in the town, what they are doing (for example walking or flying a kite), and quick-chat picks. Quick chat is made only of ready-made phrases, pictures and actions; there is no typing, voice or photos. We do not store any of it; it goes straight between the devices playing.

No ads, no tracking, no third parties

The game has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software that collects data. We do not sell or share information because we do not collect any.

Children

Sunny Town is made for children. It does not collect personal information from children. There is no free-text chat; when playing together, players can only send ready-made phrases and pictures, and playing together is off until a grown-up turns it on. Anything that leaves the game, such as a link or a purchase, sits behind a grown-up check.

Deleting data

A grown-up can delete all saved games from inside the app: Menu, then For grown-ups, then Delete all saved games. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device. iCloud data can also be managed in the device's Settings, under your Apple Account and iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
