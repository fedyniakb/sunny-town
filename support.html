Common questions

My progress isn't on my other device. Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open the game on the title screen for a moment so it can pick up the newest save.

How do I start over? Menu, then For grown-ups, then Delete all saved games. A grown-up answers a quick math question first.

Can my child talk to strangers? No. There is no chat or messaging with other people.

Are there ads or in-game purchases with real money? There are no ads. Coins are earned by playing and can't be bought.
